Privacy policy

We collect only the information required to authenticate users and display inventory tracking data. Account credentials and personal details are stored securely and are only used for system access, notifications, and administrative review.

Sensitive data, including passwords and two-factor secrets, is protected using secure hashing and token-based authentication. We do not share your private user data with third parties without explicit consent.

Data handling

You may request account removal or data export by contacting the system administrator. We retain operational log and historical tank readings only for service continuity and audit purposes.
